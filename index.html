Robotics Projects

A novel design for a soft robot using chained links and a flexible pinion-gear mechanism. Developed the manipulator from scratch - designing, manufacturing, actuator interation, and control architecture design.

Structure optimisation of Elastomeric Links of Soft Manipulator

Worked on problem on axial contorllabilty of a soft manipulator from structure perspective. Implemented Constrained Energy Minimisation to optimise for the manipulator structure.
